# Academic integration contract

The 20 canonical unit experiences are compiled consumers of Omega; they are not twenty independent handmade sites.

Before a unit can enter visual production it must resolve its canonical title, purpose and topics from the live academic authority; perform the research delta; map material claims to verified evidence; produce a content/storyboard map; and pass the applicable AG/HX gates. The web factory may enrich representation but may not silently alter curriculum, evidence, formal evaluation or rules.

Canonical unit folders in Drive retain their existing invariant of two final PDFs. HTML governance, source packages, QA and releases live in the dedicated Omega HTML governance structure.
